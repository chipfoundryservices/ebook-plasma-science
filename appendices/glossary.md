# Appendix A: Plasma Science Glossary

- **Actinometry:** Spectroscopic method using a rare-gas tracer to determine absolute radical densities in a reactive plasma.
- **Afterglow:** The temporal decay period following the cessation of RF power in a pulsed plasma discharge.
- **Bohm Criterion:** Requirement that positive ions enter the sheath with velocity equal to or greater than the ion acoustic speed $u_B = \sqrt{k_B T_e / M_i}$.
- **Capacitively Coupled Plasma (CCP):** Discharge sustained by RF electric fields between parallel conductive electrodes.
- **Child-Langmuir Law:** Formula relating space-charge-limited ion current density to applied sheath potential and sheath thickness.
- **Debye Length ($\lambda_D$):** Characteristic distance over which mobile charge carriers shield out electrostatic potentials in a plasma.
- **Dissociative Attachment:** Inelastic collision where a low-energy electron binds to a molecule and splits it into a neutral and negative ion.
- **Electronegative Plasma:** A discharge containing a significant population of negative ions relative to electrons ($\alpha = n_- / n_e \gg 1$).
- **Electron Temperature ($T_e$):** Measure of the mean kinetic energy of electrons in a plasma, conventionally expressed in electronvolts (1 eV $\approx$ 11,600 K).
- **Inductively Coupled Plasma (ICP):** High-density discharge sustained by time-varying RF magnetic fields inducing azimuthal electric currents.
- **Ion Energy Distribution Function (IEDF):** Energy spectrum of ions striking the wafer surface.
- **Optical Emission Spectroscopy (OES):** Non-invasive diagnostic analyzing emitted light wavelengths to identify chemical species and detect process endpoints.
- **Plasma Potential ($V_p$):** Electric potential of the bulk quasi-neutral plasma relative to grounded vacuum walls.
- **Sheath:** Non-neutral boundary layer between the bulk plasma and a contact surface where strong electric fields accelerate positive ions.
